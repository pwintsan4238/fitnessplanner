## Brand & Style

This design system blends **High-Contrast Athletic Performance** with **Futuristic Dark Glassmorphism**. Engineered specifically for Myanmar's dynamic mobile-first fitness market, the visual language communicates discipline, high stakes, transformation, and technological precision.

### Personality & Core Attributes
- **Electric & High-Octane:** Electric lime and deep volcanic darks channel physical stamina, explosive gym performance, and hyper-focus.
- **Prestige & Trust:** High-end workout and nutrition programming balanced with secure, culturally localized payment workflows (KBZPay, WavePay).
- **Localized Authority:** Balanced dual-script typography ensures Myanmar Unicode characters render with structural clarity alongside aggressive numeric and Latin pricing tables.

### Emotional Response
The interface must instill immediacy, personal empowerment, and frictionless commitment. Users should feel that entering the app is stepping onto an illuminated gym floor—focused, stripped of distractions, and built for results.

## Colors

The palette is engineered primarily for high-contrast dark environments to optimize battery performance on OLED mobile screens and prioritize focus.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#84CC16` - Volt Lime):** Used for primary conversion triggers, workout milestone rings, active progress states, and key metric callouts. It commands instant visual priority against obsidian backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#F97316` - Blaze Orange):** Drives promotional urgency, countdown timers, limited-time discount badges (e.g., *ဒီလအတွက် အထူးလျှော့ဈေး*), and high-intensity workout markers.
- **Tertiary & Payment Brand Accents:**
  - **KBZPay Cyan (`#005BAA` / `#1A73E8`):** Direct trust anchor for KBZPay mobile gateway modules and verification steps.
  - **Wave Money Bright Gold (`#FFD100`):** Express checkout tags and VIP status badges.
- **Neutral Core (`#030712` - Void Charcoal):** The root canvas tone. Elevated surface tiers step up to `#0F172A`, `#182234`, and `#1E293B` to establish depth without sacrificing contrast.
- **Alert & Error (`#EF4444`):** Slip upload failures, expired session notices, and cardiac warning zones.

## Typography

The typographic system provides parity across Latin numerals, Latin fitness vernacular, and Myanmar Unicode text.

### Script Pairing & Metrics
- **English & Numeric Display (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a technical, sharp edge for metric counters, workout reps, sets, and MMK price lockups.
- **Body & Burmese Typography (`Inter` paired with fallback stack `'Noto Sans Myanmar', 'Pyidaungsu', sans-serif`):**
  - Line height is boosted by 15–20% on all body styles compared to pure Latin defaults to accommodate tall Burmese ascenders, tone marks, and subscripts without clipping.
  - Never truncate Myanmar body copy with single-line ellipsis if grammatical meaning relies on terminal particles.

### Pricing Presentation Rule
For subscription tier cards:
- Previous price: `body-md` in `#64748B` with a centered strikethrough.
- Active discounted price: `headline-lg` in `#84CC16`, accompanied by the currency descriptor `label-md` (`MMK`) aligned to the baseline.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture prioritized for handheld mobile interaction where thumb access governs conversions.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns. Gutter: `1rem` (16px), Canvas Margin: `1rem` (16px). Key conversion triggers (e.g., checkout buttons) pin to the bottom edge with a blur-underlay dock.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns. Gutter: `1.5rem` (24px), Canvas Margin: `2rem` (32px). Two-column subscription cards and split checkout previews.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns. Gutter: `2rem` (32px), Canvas Margin: `3rem` (48px). Maximum container width is constrained to `1200px` for optimal scanning.

### Spacing Principles
- Compact vertical stacking within data-dense metric pods using `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- Generous separation between structural step-wizard phases and plan options using `space-xl` to maintain cognitive clarity during high-value transactions.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of translucent, tinted backdrops and electric rim lighting.

### Layer Stack
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Solid obsidian base (`#030712`).
- **Layer 1 (Card & Sub-panel Base):** Dark slate surface (`#0F172A` at 85% opacity) with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `1px` structural outline (`#1E293B`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Badges & Metric Tiles):** Mid-slate surface (`#182234` at 90% opacity), `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, bounded by a `1px` rim border (`#334155`).
- **Layer 3 (Modal, Floating Bar, Bottom Sheet):** `#0F172A` at 95% opacity with an upper border stroke of `#334155` and a soft ambient glow: `0 -8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

### Volt Illumination Technique
Featured subscription cards and active workout tiles implement a perimeter glow:
- Outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.25)`.
- Border highlight: `1px solid rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The geometric approach uses controlled, modern radii (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline) conveying structural athletic discipline rather than child-like playfulness.

- **Standard Buttons, Inputs & Metric Tiles:** `0.5rem` (8px). Keeps edges clean and precise.
- **Cards, Wizards & Slips Dropzones (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px). Creates soft, ergonomic structural bounds for dense blocks of information.
- **Hero Promotional Panels & Modals (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Metric Chips, Language Selectors, and Badges:** Fully rounded pill format (`9999px`) to immediately separate secondary status tags from actionable primary panels.
